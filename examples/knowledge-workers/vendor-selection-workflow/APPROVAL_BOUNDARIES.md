# Approval Boundaries

## Ask-First Conditions

- Any use of non-approved external sources.
- Any statement with legal or compliance implications.
- Any distribution outside the working group.
- Any final recommendation sent to leadership.
- Any promotion from internal draft to leadership pilot.
- Any promotion from leadership pilot to operational use.
- Any override of a blocking security or adoption gate.
- Any decision to ignore an active rollback trigger.

## Required Signoffs

- Stakeholder owner signoff on draft.
- Director signoff before leadership distribution.
- Stakeholder owner signoff before leadership pilot.
- Stakeholder owner and director signoff before operational use.
- Incident owner signoff before rollback closure.
